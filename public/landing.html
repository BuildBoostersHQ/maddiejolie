About
Why I do this.

My grandfather ran a carpet store in Brooklyn for 30 years. I spent weekends there watching him answer the phone, write up quotes, and earn customers one handshake at a time. That's where I learned what it actually looks like to run a small business.

I started building websites for my church, then for family, then for friends who needed help getting found online. Eventually I ended up at GoHighLevel, the software behind a lot of the marketing systems agencies sell to small businesses for thousands a month. I saw what worked, what didn't, and how much contractors were overpaying for it.

So now I do it myself, directly, for people like you. No agency markup, no six-month contract, no runaround. Just a website that makes you look as good as your work already is, and a system that makes sure the phone keeps ringing.
